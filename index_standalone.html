科技與現代東亞的歷史共構

The Historical Coproduction of Techno-science and Modern East Asia

Introduction

關於本工作坊

工作坊基本構想

本工作坊的目標，是在「東亞科技史」的專史基礎之上，進一步探究科技與醫療如何參與塑造近代東亞的形貌。換言之，我們將以科技與醫療為核心，探索東亞近代史的新視野。

首先，我們將借重「科技與社會」研究（Science, Technology and Society; STS）的核心洞察：科技不應被視為獨立於社會文化之外的自足領域，學者應探究科技發展過程中與之「共構」而生的社會與文化現象。

其次，我們將科技與社會「共構」的洞察轉化為歷史研究的視野，進一步探索目前國際STS學界較少關注的長程歷史性問題：東亞各國在容受與發展近代科技的歷史過程中，究竟萌生出哪些具體而特殊的社會、文化、體制與價值？除了科學史研究中成果豐碩的主題，如國家建構、經濟發展、殖民統治、環境改造、性別差異、基礎建設與現代性等之外，我們也將致力於拓展視野至科學史學者較少關注、但對東亞現代史極為重要的議題，例如「天與自然」、文明論述、普世主義、道德權威、戰爭動員、生命情感與精神性等。

透過重新追問近代史的一系列基本問題，我們希望探索科技在東亞「近代歷史巨變」中尚未充分彰顯的角色。這不僅有助於更具體地理解東亞科技的歷史形貌，也能反向促進東亞科技史的進一步發展，乃至參與當前蓬勃發展中的科技全球史（The Global History of Science）之建設。

工作坊具體形式：圓桌論壇

本工作坊原為慶祝近史所七十週年所籌劃的「近代史的新視野」系列會議之一，後納入近史所與二十世紀中國史學會合辦的國際會議「重構跨越台海的近代史：交纏、分歧與全球連結」（2026年8月4–6日）之中，並於會議結束次日（8月7日）另行舉辦本單日工作坊。

本工作坊將採取「圓桌論壇」的形式，邀請學者從他們自身長期關懷的研究領域出發，簡要說明科技在其中可能扮演、但尚未充分受到重視的角色，發言時間約 7–10 分鐘，希望藉此邀請在場學者共同發想並開啟更為深入的討論。

除三場圓桌論壇之外，另安排一場討論與本工作坊主題密切相關的東亞科學史論文專輯：Science Wars before the "Science Wars": East Asia as Method（Historia Scientiarum 34(2)）。歡迎有興趣的朋友事先閱讀該專輯導論。

工作坊最後亦將討論如何建設以東亞為基地的科學史與STS學術網絡，包括與 History of Science Society 的合作，以及將於2027年在台灣舉辦的 Society for the Social Studies of Science 年會（4S）。

Conceptual Framework

This workshop builds on the existing field of the history of science and technology in East Asia, and aims to investigate more broadly how science, technology, and medicine participated in shaping the contours of modern East Asia. In short, it explores new perspectives on modern East Asian history through the lens of science, technology, and medicine.

We draw on the core insight of Science, Technology and Society (STS) studies: science and technology should not be treated as a self-contained domain independent of society and culture. Scholars should investigate the social and cultural phenomena that are "coproduced" with technological development.

We translate this STS insight into a historical lens to explore questions rarely addressed in international STS scholarship: what specific social, cultural, institutional, and moral formations emerged as East Asian countries received and developed modern technoscience? Beyond well-established topics in the history of science — such as state-building, economic development, colonial governance, environmental transformation, gender, infrastructure, and modernity — we also seek to expand into themes less commonly studied by historians of science but central to modern East Asian history, including "Tian and Nature," discourses of civilization, universalism, moral authority, wartime mobilization, and vitality and spirituality.

By revisiting foundational questions of modern history, we hope to illuminate the underexplored role of technoscience in the great modern transformations of East Asia — deepening our understanding of the historical contours of East Asian technoscience, and contributing to the growing field of the Global History of Science.

Format: Roundtable Discussions

This workshop was originally planned as part of the series "New Perspectives on Modern History," organized in celebration of the 70th anniversary of the Institute of Modern History, Academia Sinica. It was subsequently incorporated into the international conference "Restructuring Modern History across the Taiwan Strait: Entanglement, Divergence, and Global Connections" (4–6 August 2026), co-organized by the Institute of Modern History and the Association for the History of Modern China — and is held the following day as a dedicated one-day workshop.

Using a roundtable format, each scholar will speak for approximately 7–10 minutes from their own research area, briefly sketching the role that technoscience may have played but has not yet received sufficient attention. This is intended to invite collective reflection and open deeper discussion.

In addition to three roundtable sessions, there will be a dedicated discussion of the journal special issue Science Wars before the "Science Wars": East Asia as Method (Historia Scientiarum 34(2)). Participants are welcome to read the introduction to the special issue in advance.

The final session will address the development of East Asia-based scholarly networks in the history of science and STS, including cooperation with the History of Science Society (HSS) and preparations for the 4S (Society for the Social Studies of Science) annual conference in Taiwan in 2027.
